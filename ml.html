Computational Workflow

From Chemical Structure to Predictive Intelligence

How our AI models translate molecular SMILES and optical inputs into reliable physical properties in milliseconds.

1
Molecular Encoding

Chemical structures are parsed via RDKit into canonical SMILES and converted into high-dimensional Daylight, Morgan, or MACCS bit vectors.

2
Deep Neural Inference

Trained multilayer perceptrons (MLPs) and ensemble neural architectures evaluate complex non-linear electronic interactions without costly DFT runs.

3
Actionable Predictions

Researchers receive instantaneous predictions for PCE%, absorption maxima, or pH metrics to prioritize synthetic candidate targets in the wet lab.
