About Me

Scientist, Educator & Innovator

Currently a Researcher at the Institute of Nanoscience and Nanotechnology (INN), NCSR "Demokritos," Athens — and a faculty member at Al-Azhar University, Cairo.

My interdisciplinary approach fuses solid-state synthesis, crystal structure determination, high-level DFT calculations, and AI-driven discovery to address global challenges in clean energy and environmental remediation.

Athens, Greece & Cairo, Egypt

Dr. Mohamed Mahmoud Elsenety is a chemist and materials scientist whose work spans the full chain from molecule to device. He holds a Ph.D. from the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens and has held postdoctoral positions in Greece and Taiwan (NTHU).

His research integrates experimental and theoretical approaches — solid-state and solution-phase synthesis, crystal structure determination, Rietveld analysis, DFT calculations using CASTEP, Gaussian, and Quantum ESPRESSO, molecular docking via MOE, and advanced characterization (XRD, IR, NMR, mass spectrometry, TGA/DTA/DSC, SEM, AFM, TEM, XPS, PL spectroscopy).

He also employs artificial intelligence, machine learning, and deep learning to guide the design and optimization of functional materials, bridging computational modeling with real-world applications in solar energy, sensing, and water purification.
