Privacy Policy

Guidelines governing personal information handling, cookies, and privacy ethics across this academic portfolio and associated computational tools.

Last Updated: October 2026 URL: https://elsenety.github.io/

1. Academic Scope & Purpose

This personal academic website is operated by Dr. Mohamed Elsenety to present research activities, peer-reviewed publications, open-access machine learning web tools, and academic credentials. We are committed to protecting the privacy of visitors, students, and academic collaborators.

2. Information Collection

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver and improve our site:

  • Direct Inquiries: When you submit a message via the Contact page form, your provided name, institutional affiliation, email address, and message content are securely transmitted to facilitate academic correspondence.
  • Aggregated Web Metrics: Non-personally identifiable technical parameters (e.g., browser type, approximate geographic region, page view frequency) may be processed to assess traffic to published scientific tools.

3. Local Storage & Cookies

This website does not employ commercial advertising cookies or cross-site tracking scripts. Browser localStorage is used exclusively for functional user interface preferences, such as retaining your preferred visual display mode (Light or Dark theme).

4. External Services & Scientific Repositories

This site links to trusted external scientific platforms, including Google Scholar, ORCID, Scopus, Nature Publishing Group, ScienceDirect, Google Play Store, and Streamlit Cloud. When following external hyperlinks, visitors are subject to the respective privacy terms of those services.

5. Data Confidentiality

We do not sell, rent, or monetize personal contact information. Information shared with us is used strictly for academic collaboration, peer review, or answering direct inquiries.
